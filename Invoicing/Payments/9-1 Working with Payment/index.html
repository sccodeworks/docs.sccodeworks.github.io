Working With Payments​

Task Description​

The WDLS -Finance package offers a flexible cash application function. The Working With Payment option allows the user several methods to post checks and review cash applications made to particular invoices. The customer payments can be applied to one invoice in full, to partially pay an invoice, to pay a few invoices, or a combination of invoices.

The program also allows the user to create batches of customer payments and will print a deposit slip of these batches for banking purposes.
